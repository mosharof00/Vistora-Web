# Vistora landing requirements (source of truth for later work)

Do not implement this entire list in the current pass. Keep it here for future prompts.

**Brand:** Vistora Tours & Travels  
**Tagline:** Explore More, Travel Beyond

## Purpose

- Introduce the company and build trust
- Attract domestic and international travelers
- Capture leads for air ticket, visa, tour, work permit, and other travel services
- Convert Facebook / Instagram / WhatsApp traffic
- Make WhatsApp, phone, and email contact immediate

## Design

Modern, premium, clean, corporate, travel-focused, mobile-friendly, fast.

## Header / navigation

Sticky header. Logo left. Links: Home, About Us, Services, Tours, Visa, Contact. CTA: WhatsApp Us or Book / Enquire Now. Hamburger on mobile.

## Sections (full site)

1. About Vistora (+ Read More)
2. Our Services (cards + Enquire Now): Air Ticketing, Visa Processing, Tour Packages, Umrah & Hajj, Work Permit, Hotel Booking, Travel Insurance, Airport Transfer, Travel Consultation
3. Popular Destinations (Malaysia, Thailand, Singapore, UAE, Saudi Arabia, India, Maldives, Turkey, Azerbaijan, Georgia) — admin can add/remove later
4. Tour Packages (destination, name, duration, starting price, image, short description, view details, enquire)
5. Visa Assistance by country — never claim “visa approval guaranteed”
6. Why Choose Vistora
7. How It Works (Contact → Plan → Confirm → Travel)
8. Customer reviews — admin CRUD later
9. Photo gallery — admin upload later
10. Inquiry form (name*, mobile*, email, destination*, travel date, travelers, service type, message) → thank-you state
11. Floating WhatsApp (prefill: “Hello Vistora Tours & Travels, I would like to know more about your travel services.”)
12. Floating Call on mobile
13. Socials: only official accounts that exist (Facebook today: facebook.com/VistoraTravels)
14. Footer with quick links, contact, licenses, © 2026

## Admin / leads (later)

- CRUD: packages, destinations, visas, services, gallery, reviews, homepage banner
- Inquiry inbox with status: New → Contacted → Processing → Completed → Cancelled

## Quality

Mobile-first across phone, tablet, laptop, desktop. Image optimization (WebP/AVIF), lazy loading, minified assets, caching/CDN as needed.
